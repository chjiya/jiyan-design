Common Questions

Your Dental Questions, Answered

Transparency is part of our promise. Here's what patients most often ask us.

Does dental work hurt? I have a low pain tolerance. +
Not at Parkview. We offer multiple sedation options (nitrous oxide, oral sedation, and IV sedation) to ensure you're completely comfortable. Our team is trained in pain-free numbing techniques — we use a slow, warmed injection that patients barely feel. Most patients tell us "that was nothing like I expected."
What insurance do you accept? How much will I pay? +
We are in-network with all major PPO plans including Delta Dental, MetLife, Cigna, Aetna, Guardian, Humana, United Concordia, and Principal. For a specific cost estimate, call our team — we'll run your benefits and give you a clear breakdown before your first appointment. No surprises, ever.
How often should I come in for a checkup? +
We recommend a checkup and professional cleaning every 6 months. Regular visits let us catch small issues before they become big (and expensive) problems. Patients on our membership plan get two cleanings per year included, making it easy to stay on track.
Do you see children? What about emergency patients? +
Yes — we treat patients of all ages, from toddlers to seniors. And yes, we reserve same-day emergency slots every day. Call (212) 555-0192 and we'll get you in the same day. Our emergency fee is transparent and we never bill you extra for weekend urgency.
How long does teeth whitening take to show results? +
Our in-office Zoom® whitening takes about 90 minutes and you'll leave 6–10 shades whiter. For take-home kits, you'll see noticeable results in 5–7 days. We always do a sensitivity assessment beforehand and use desensitizing agents to keep you comfortable throughout.
