## 4. 6 句英文话术

> 接单：I'd love to help with your project. I have experience building AI-powered landing pages for small businesses.
> 沟通：Could you share a few examples of websites you like? That helps me understand your taste.
> 报价：For a full landing page with AI automation, my rate is $399. That includes design, development, and 2 rounds of revisions.
> 交付：Your website is live. I've also set up an auto-responder for contact form submissions. Test it out!
> 好评：If you're happy, a 5-star review on Upwork would mean a lot to me as a new freelancer.
