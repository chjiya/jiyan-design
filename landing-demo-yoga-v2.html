Our Way

Three paths, one intention

Whether you're new to the mat or returning after a break — we meet you where you are.

🌊

Vinyasa Flow

Dynamic sequences linking breath to movement. Build strength, heat, and focus. All levels welcome.

🌲

Hatha Basics

Slower, more deliberate. Hold poses longer, find alignment, build foundation. Perfect for beginners.

🌙

Yin Yoga

Deep, passive stretches held for 3-5 minutes. Release fascia, calm the nervous system. End with tea.
