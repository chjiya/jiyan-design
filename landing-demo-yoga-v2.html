Our Story

A Space to Breathe Deep

Founded in 2018, Sunrise Yoga was born from a simple belief: yoga should meet you where you are, not the other way around. Our sunlit studio in Williamsburg is a sanctuary from the noise of the city — a place where you can move, breathe, and reconnect.

Whether you're stepping onto the mat for the first time or you've been practicing for decades, our doors are open. We offer three distinct paths — Vinyasa Flow, Hatha Basics, and Yin — each taught by teachers who see you, not just your practice.

Our Way

Three Paths, One Intention

Whether you're new to the mat or returning after a break — we meet you where you are.

🌊

Vinyasa Flow

Dynamic & Energizing

Dynamic sequences linking breath to movement. Build strength, heat, and focus. All levels welcome — modifications offered for every pose.

🌲

Hatha Basics

Grounded & Intentional

Slower, more deliberate. Hold poses longer, find alignment, build foundation. Perfect for beginners and anyone craving a deeper connection to each asana.

🌙

Yin Yoga

Deep & Restorative

Deep, passive stretches held for 3–5 minutes. Release fascia, calm the nervous system, and settle into stillness. End each session with warm tea.
